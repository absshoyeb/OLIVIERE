Black Truffle Risotto

A creamy Italian classic finished with aged parmesan and delicate black truffle.

Slow-cooked arborio rice is prepared with rich stock, butter, and herbs, creating a refined dish with deep aroma and elegant flavor.

Seared Atlantic Salmon

Perfectly seared salmon served with citrus butter and seasonal vegetables.

Light, fresh, and beautifully balanced, this dish brings together crisp texture, bright flavor, and a luxurious finish.

Charcoal-Grilled Wagyu Steak

Premium wagyu grilled over charcoal and served with herb butter.

Rich, tender, and full of character, this signature steak is crafted for guests who appreciate bold flavor and fine technique.

Lobster Ravioli

Handmade pasta filled with lobster and finished in a creamy saffron sauce.

A refined seafood favorite, combining delicate pasta, rich lobster filling, and smooth aromatic sauce.

Dark Chocolate Soufflé

A warm, airy dessert made with rich dark chocolate and vanilla cream.

Light on the outside and deeply indulgent within, this dessert is a graceful ending to the Olivière dining experience.
